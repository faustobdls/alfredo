# Instruction review checklist

## Intent and scope

- Is the intended outcome explicit, observable, and bounded?
- Are triggers, anti-triggers, and named handoffs distinct from nearby catalog
  resources?

## Operational behaviour

- Are decisions, questions, and stopping conditions testable?
- Does the artifact define what happens with missing evidence or failed tools?
- Is the output shape clear enough for a caller to use?

## Safety and portability

- Are tools actually available and appropriate to the role?
- Are credentials, destructive actions, remote mutations, and provider-specific
  assumptions contained?
- Are paths relative and references local to the installed skill?

## Catalogue fit

- Does the canonical path and frontmatter match the artifact type?
- Is package membership, versioning, and changelog coverage correct?
- Does the resource preserve Alfredo's voice without duplicating another method?
